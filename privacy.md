# gmail-spark privacy policy

gmail-spark is a personal, self-hosted application used only by its developer, to archive the developer's own Gmail messages.

**Data accessed.** The app requests the read-only Gmail scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) for the developer's own Google account and reads that mailbox's messages.

**Data storage.** Retrieved messages and the OAuth tokens are stored only on the developer's own self-hosted server. Nothing is stored on any third-party service.

**Data sharing.** The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. It is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

**Other users.** The app is not offered to other people and does not collect data from anyone else.

**Deletion.** Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the locally stored data can be deleted by the developer.
